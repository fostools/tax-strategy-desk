# Tax Strategy Desk

Ready-made 2026 US federal tax strategy plans by age and income, with a live baseline calculator and a step-by-step playbook for every strategy.

**Live site:** https://fostools.github.io/tax-strategy-desk/

Scenarios included: age 25 · $25k, 30 · $75k, 35 · $200k, 40 · $1M, 50 · $2M.

Figures are based on IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, Notice 2025-67 and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (July 2025).

> Educational information only, not tax, legal or investment advice. Confirm anything you act on with a CPA or tax attorney.
